# PTTini housing – change log

---

## v1.1 – 2026-11-23

**Reprint both halves.** v1.0 and v1.1 halves don't fit together: the joint heights have changed.

**Electret**
- Capsule sits 0.5 mm higher, centred in the housing.
- The full cups are replaced by four corner pads (about 60% grip), with a 0.5 mm gap round the capsule.
- The lead opening has moved up with the capsule.
- The front joint and the three mic slots are now centred on the capsule.

**PCB mounting**
- The PCB sits 0.5 mm higher, giving 2.0 mm under the board for through-hole leads.
- The jack hole and rear joint have moved up with it, so the jack is still centred.
- Bottom boss reduced from Ø9 to Ø8, for clearance to component legs.
- New: cross supports under the PTT switch and under the jack, so the board is supported when the PTT is pressed or a plug is inserted.

**Screw boss (top half)**
- Boss enlarged from Ø6.2 to Ø7; pilot hole reduced from Ø2.6 to Ø2.4.
- New: two ribs tie the boss to the side walls, keeping at least 2 mm clear of components.
- Use a 10 mm M3 self-tapper (8 mm is no longer recommended).

**PTT**
- Button hole enlarged from Ø4.3 to Ø4.5.
- The button now stands about 0.5 mm proud of the dish rim.

**Print settings**
- Use at least 4 perimeters (was 3).

**Files**
- Top, bottom and assembly STEP files updated.
- PCB STEP: the board and electret are placed 0.5 mm higher to match the housing. The board design is unchanged.
